01Guiding principles

Integrity.
Open, transparent, humble in our words and action.
Founder first.
Here to serve the best outcomes for founders with a give-first mentality. We are here to help, not to lecture.
Discretion.
Vigilant not to share information beyond the group without checking first.
Diversity.
Dedicated to keeping the group as diverse as possible across as many dimensions as possible.

02Outcomes we want

  • Growth of the Irish ecosystem.
  • Growth in the level and variety of angel investment in Ireland.
  • An increase in the global ambition of Irish startups.
  • Better connections between Irish founders and the best-placed investor, mentor or coach from the wide range of operators in Ireland with current or previous multinational experience.
  • Nurturing ideas and talent that can go under-represented in the established investment community.

03Code of conduct between members

  1. We're all grown-ups; we will be bureaucracy-light, with a strong bias to only establishing policies when absolutely necessary.
  2. Members do not give financial advice to each other in a professional capacity.
  3. Members do not sell to each other.
  4. Members do not sell to or solicit work from founders the group interacts with. This does not preclude founders inviting Tech Operators members to become advisors or board members.

04Terms and conditions

  1. No advice

    1. In this clause, “Tech Operators” means Tech Operators and its board, and “Member” means any individual member of the Tech Operators network.
    2. Tech Operators is a network of experienced executives, founders and angel investors who advise, connect and invest in ambitious companies. Tech Operators is not authorised or regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland or any other regulatory authority, and nothing in its activities is intended to constitute a regulated financial service.
    3. Nothing said, written, presented or otherwise provided by Tech Operators or any Member, whether to a company it works with or to another Member, constitutes or is intended to constitute:
      1. investment advice or investment research, or a personal recommendation in relation to any security, financial instrument or investment;
      2. financial, accounting, taxation or actuarial advice;
      3. legal advice; or
      4. any other form of professional or regulated advice.
    4. Any guidance, opinion, introduction, mentoring, feedback or other input provided by Tech Operators or a Member is general in nature, is shared on an informal basis drawing on personal experience, and is provided for information and discussion purposes only. It must not be relied upon as a substitute for independent professional advice.
    5. Each Member acts in a personal capacity and not as a representative, agent or adviser of Tech Operators, of any other Member, or of any employer or other organisation with which the Member is associated. The views of a Member are the Member’s own.
    6. Where a decision may have financial, investment, legal, tax or other professional implications, the recipient should obtain its own advice from an appropriately qualified and, where relevant, authorised professional adviser before acting. Each recipient remains solely responsible for its own decisions and for evaluating the merits and risks of any course of action.
    7. To the fullest extent permitted by law, neither Tech Operators nor any Member accepts any liability for any loss or damage arising from reliance on any guidance, opinion, introduction or other input provided in connection with the Tech Operators network.
