Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Tech Operators collects through techoperators.ie and how we use it.

Draft for board review. The current site's privacy page is password-protected. This plain-English draft covers what the new forms collect and needs checking before launch.

On this page

  2. How we use it
  3. Where it is stored
  4. How long we keep it

01What we collect

When you apply as a founder or apply for membership, we collect the details you enter in the form, such as your name, email address, company, website, LinkedIn profile and any message you include. Member profiles in the directory are written and managed by the members themselves.

02How we use it

We use your details to consider your application, to contact you about it and, if you opt in, to send you news and updates. We do not sell your information.

03Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored with our website host (Netlify) and in our contact management system (Attio). Member directory data is stored in Google Workspace.

04How long we keep it

We keep application details for as long as needed to handle your application and maintain a record of our relationship with you, and delete them on request.

05Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing events@techoperators.ie. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission.
