Mentorship: we're here to support great founders

The Tech Operators mentor programme is our flagship initiative, designed to provide founders with a safe space to explore challenges, accelerate international growth and scale your business efficiently. It represents our commitment to our core operating principle of always being "founder first".

You enter the programme following a selection process run by our board, and successfully matching with one of our members as a mentor after chemistry and introduction calls. Founders and mentors are encouraged to meet regularly, discuss and agree outcomes at the outset and document the biggest challenges they discuss together.

The strength of the programme lies in the deep expertise and real-world operator experience of our members, as well as the connections a mentor can make for founders, either from their personal network or within the Tech Operators network.

A Meitheal: a room of operators focused on your challenge

  • One specific problem
  • Honest, confidential advice
  • How a Meitheal works →

A Meitheal is a curated group of Tech Operators members who come together to help a founder tackle one specific challenge in their business, and leave them with fresh ideas, warm introductions and a clearer path forward.

Meitheal is an Irish word for neighbours coming together to work, each bringing what they can.

Not a pitch. A Meitheal isn't an investment meeting. It's time with operators who have faced the same problem, focused entirely on yours.

Expertise
The Meitheal draws on the expertise of the group to help founders solve a problem in their business.
Respect
We respect founders by keeping their data confidential and by being honest, straight-talking and free of self-interest in our advice.
Global matchmakers
We open our address books, especially our international ones, to do our utmost to help Irish companies scale beyond Ireland.
Generosity of spirit
We are positive, growth-oriented and filled with ambition for scaling Irish startups.

Pitch to people who've built it before

Our members are angels who have built and scaled technology companies, so you're pitching to people who understand what you're building and can bring experience and introductions as well as capital.

How our members invest

Tech Operators isn't a fund or a syndicate. Each member decides individually whether to invest and writes their own cheque. Most investments are made through SAFE notes, but members have also invested in priced equity rounds and through convertible loan notes, both alongside a lead VC and without one. Pitching doesn't guarantee investment, but every founder leaves with feedback from experienced operators.

Who it's for

Pre-seed and seed-stage startups based in Ireland or with a strong Irish connection, building software with a B2B or highly tech-enabled model, and ambitious to grow internationally. We're particularly keen to hear from founders who don't already have a network of investors to call on.
