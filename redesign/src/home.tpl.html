Why Tech Operators?

Our network represents years of real-life experience, earned at major multinational companies and successful Irish startups and scaleups. Our members are current and former senior executives at companies including Google, Meta, Salesforce, Intercom, Wayflyer, Stripe, Microsoft, Docusign, OpenAI, Lattice, Personio and Oracle. We've worked across every function from GTM to engineering, finance to operations.

But experience only matters if it's put to work. We don't sit on the sidelines. When you work with Tech Operators, you tap into people who've solved similar problems and who'll draw on their experience and open their network to help you solve it.

That might mean pitching to our members for angel investment. It might mean a Meitheal: a room of operators focused entirely on a challenge you bring, leaving you with fresh ideas, warm introductions and a clear path forward. Or it might mean an ongoing relationship with a member who's been where you want to go.
