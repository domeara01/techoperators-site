Meitheal principles

A Meitheal is a curated group of members who come together to help a founder tackle a specific challenge in their business.

Expertise

The Meitheal draws on the expertise of the group to help founders solve a problem in their business. We acknowledge that founders are not looking for investment at the Meitheal, or even a full endorsement of their idea.

Respect

We respect founders by keeping their data confidential and by being honest, straight-talking and free of self-interest in our advice.

Global matchmakers

We open our address books, especially our international ones, to do our utmost to help Irish companies scale beyond Ireland.

Generosity of spirit

A huge part of what we can do is help founders push the boulder up the hill. We are positive, growth-oriented and filled with ambition for scaling Irish startups.
