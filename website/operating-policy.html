Operating policy

Guiding principles

  • Integrity. Open, transparent, humble in our words and action.
  • Founder first. Here to serve the best outcomes for founders with a give-first mentality. We are here to help, not to lecture.
  • Discretion. Vigilant not to share information beyond the group without checking first.
  • Diversity. Dedicated to keeping the group as diverse as possible across as many dimensions as possible.

Outcomes we want

  • Growth of the Irish ecosystem.
  • Growth in the level and variety of angel investment in Ireland.
  • An increase in the global ambition of Irish startups.
  • Better connections between Irish founders and the best-placed investor, mentor or coach from the wide range of operators in Ireland with current or previous multinational experience.
  • Nurturing ideas and talent that can go under-represented in the established investment community.

Code of conduct between members

  • We're all grown-ups; we will be bureaucracy-light, with a strong bias to only establishing policies when absolutely necessary.
  • Members do not give financial advice to each other in a professional capacity.
  • Members do not sell to each other.
  • Members do not sell to or solicit work from founders the group interacts with. This does not preclude founders inviting Tech Operators members to become advisors or board members.
